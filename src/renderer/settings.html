About PAWSE

v1.0.0

PAWSE transforms the Pomodoro into a cozy, stress-free ritual. Choose your cat to set your pace, and while you focus, your buddy quietly does their own thing. When your timer ends, a gentle chime lets you know it is time to stretch, play, and interact with your awakened companion. Enjoy steadier progress and refreshing breaks.

Meet Your Companions

Tux

A polite cat who keeps your work steady. He is easily trained and loves routine, making him perfect to stay on track.

Ginger

A lovable goofball running on a single brain cell. He snoozes through work, saving his crazy energy and loud meows for breaks.

Void

A serious mastermind who looks like they can scratch, but is loyal. He demands total focus and silence to plan his cat domination.
